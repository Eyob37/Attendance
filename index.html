Built for busy classrooms

Thoughtful features that save teachers time and give schools reliable attendance data.

Unlimited classes

Create any number of classes with grade, section, and academic year — each with its own student roster.

One student at a time

A focused attendance card shows one student with big Present / Absent buttons and live counters.

Rich records

Search, filter, and edit attendance by class, student, date range, and status — grouped by session.

Print-ready reports

Daily, weekly, monthly, and individual reports with signature lines — perfect for printing or PDF.

Keep marking even without internet. Changes queue locally and sync automatically when you reconnect.

Real-time sync across devices with duplicate-safe writes and safe merging of remote updates.
